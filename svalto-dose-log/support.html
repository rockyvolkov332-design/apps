How do I cancel?

Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Svalto Dose Log. The cycle pass doesn't renew, so there's nothing to cancel.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple ID you paid with.

My clinic changed a dose

Open the medication and change the dose from today; the earlier doses stay in the history. Or scan the new instructions and check each line before saving.

Medical questions

Svalto Dose Log is an organiser, not medical advice. Always follow your clinic's instructions and ask your clinic about doses.
