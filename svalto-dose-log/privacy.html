Privacy policy

Svalto Dose Log · Updated 27 November 2026

The short version

Svalto Dose Log keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics of ours and no ad trackers. We never see your cycle, medications, doses or injection sites. On Android, the part of the app that reads a scanned sheet is made by Google and sends Google technical diagnostics; see "Camera and photos".

What the app stores

Your cycle type and dates, clinic name, medications, doses and dose changes, the doses you log with times and injection sites, and your reminder settings. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

Camera and photos

If you scan your clinic's instructions, the photo or screenshot is read on your device. The image and its text are never uploaded, and the app does not keep the image in its own storage.

On iPhone the text is read with Apple's on-device text recognition and, where available, on-device Apple Intelligence.

On Android the text is read with Google's on-device ML Kit text recognition. ML Kit works on the phone, but it sends Google technical information for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (such as model and Android version), the app's package name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your phone, performance figures, feature events and error codes. It is sent encrypted. It does not include your photo, its text, or anything you enter in the app. Details: Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

What we collect

We collect nothing. No name, email, location, contacts or health data leave your phone, and nothing is sent to us. On Android, the ML Kit diagnostics described above go to Google, not to us.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. The store tells the app whether you have Pro; we don't receive your payment details.

Reminders

Dose and trigger-shot reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS or Android. Their text never leaves the device.

Backups

If you use iCloud, Google or computer backups, your phone may include the app's data in your own backup, covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone.
