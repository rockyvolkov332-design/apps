Privacy policy

Halve · Updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Halve keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ad trackers, so we never see your logs, your plan or your cravings.

What the app stores

The numbers you enter (strength, pouches a day, can price), your step-down plan, the pouches and cravings you log, your reminder times and your reasons for quitting. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. No name, email, location, contacts, health data or device identifiers. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). The store tells the app whether you have an active subscription; we don't receive your payment details.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the operating system. Their text never leaves the device.

Backups

If you use iCloud, Google or computer backups, your phone may include the app's data in your own backup. That backup belongs to you and is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all my data deletes everything at once. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Halve is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
