Privacy policy

Halve · Updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Everything you enter in Halve stays on your phone. There are no accounts or servers, so we never see your logs, your plan or your cravings. From version 1.0.1, the one thing that can leave the phone is ad measurement: whether the app was installed and whether a trial or purchase was made. You can switch that off.

What the app stores

The numbers you enter (strength, pouches a day, can price), your step-down plan, the pouches and cravings you log, your reminder times and your reasons for quitting. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

What we collect

We don't collect your name, email, location or contacts, and nothing you log is sent to us or to anyone else.

Ad measurement (version 1.0.1 and later)

To see whether our ads work, Halve uses Meta's App Events software (Meta Platforms, Inc.). When it is on, Meta receives:

  • that the app was installed and opened;
  • that a free trial, subscription or purchase started, with the product, its price and the store's transaction ID;
  • technical details about the device, such as its model, operating system, IP address and an advertising or app identifier.

Meta never receives your strengths, pouch logs, cravings, plan, reasons or reminder times. On iPhone we don't ask to track you across other companies' apps.

Ad measurement is off in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland. Everywhere else it is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › Ad measurement. Meta handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android). The store tells the app whether you have an active subscription; we don't receive your payment details.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the operating system. Their text never leaves the device.

Backups

If you use iCloud, Google or computer backups, your phone may include the app's data in your own backup. That backup belongs to you and is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase all my data deletes everything at once. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Halve is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.
