Halve

Step down. Then step off.

Halve helps adults quit nicotine pouches by halving the strength every couple of weeks (6 → 3 → 1.5 mg → nicotine-free) on a schedule they choose. Log every pouch with one tap, ride out cravings with SOS, and see the money you kept.

No account. Everything you log stays on your phone. For adults 18+. Halve is a habit tracker, not medical advice or treatment.

Available on the App Store and Google Play.
