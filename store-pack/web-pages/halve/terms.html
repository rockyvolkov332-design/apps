Who can use Halve

You must be 18 or older. Halve is for adults who already use nicotine pouches and want to stop.

Halve is a habit tracker. It helps you plan a step-down, log pouches and cravings, and see your progress. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it doesn't replace a doctor or pharmacist.
