How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Halve. Android: Play Store › profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Halve. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open Halve › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. With Pro, open Plan › Edit to change any step's strength, length or daily limit.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. If you delete the app or use Settings › Erase all my data, it's gone. We can't recover it because we never had it.

Need help quitting?

Halve is a habit tracker, not medical advice. A doctor or pharmacist can talk you through your options. In the US you can call the free quit line 1-800-QUIT-NOW.
