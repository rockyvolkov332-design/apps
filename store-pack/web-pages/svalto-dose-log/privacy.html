Privacy policy

Svalto Dose Log · Updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Svalto Dose Log keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ad trackers. We never see your cycle, medications, doses or injection sites.

What the app stores

Your cycle type and dates, clinic name, medications, doses and dose changes, the doses you log with times and injection sites, and your reminder settings. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

Camera and photos

If you scan your clinic's instructions, the photo or screenshot is read on your device using Apple's on-device text recognition and, where available, on-device Apple Intelligence. The image and its text are never uploaded, and the app does not save the image in its own storage (iOS clears its temporary copy).

What we collect

Nothing. No name, email, location, contacts, health data or device identifiers leave your phone.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. Apple tells the app whether you have Pro; we don't receive your payment details.

Reminders

Dose and trigger-shot reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS. Their text never leaves the device.

Backups

If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include the app's data in your own backup, covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone.
