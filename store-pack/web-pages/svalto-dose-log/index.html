Svalto Dose Log

Every shot, on the minute.

An organiser for IVF and egg-freezing injections: every dose for the day in order, every dose change on record, trigger-shot reminders, injection-site rotation, your cycle on one timeline, and on-device scanning of your clinic's instruction sheet.

It records what your clinic tells you. It never suggests or changes a dose. No account; everything stays on your phone.

Available on the App Store.
