Who can use Afterday

You must be 18 or older.

What it is

Afterday is a log and organiser for your recovery. It records what your surgeon told you and what you measured. It is not a medical device, and it is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It never tells you when a drain can come out, which garment to wear or what to take. Always follow your surgeon's instructions. If something worries you, call your clinic. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Recovery Pass and Monthly

The Recovery Pass is a single payment that unlocks every feature for 90 days and does not renew. Monthly is an auto-renewing subscription, billed by the App Store or Google Play at the price shown until you cancel in your store account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Apple's or Google's refund policy.

Free features

The day count, the timeline and your plan stay free, with one drain, two medications and two photos.

Your data

Everything you enter stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

No guarantee

We work to keep the app accurate and available, but it is provided as is. Check what you enter; the totals and reports are only as accurate as your entries.
