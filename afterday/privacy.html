Privacy policy

Afterday · Updated 28 November 2026

The short version

Afterday keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics and no ad trackers. We never see your surgery details, drain amounts, medications, notes or photos.

What the app stores

The procedures you choose, your surgery date and clinic name, your drain entries, your medications and the doses you tick off, the dates in your plan, your daily check (garment, walking, massage, swelling, pain, notes), your recovery photos and your reminder settings. All of it is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

Camera and photos

When you take a recovery photo, or choose one from your library, Afterday saves a copy inside its own storage on your phone. It does not add photos to your camera roll and never uploads them. Deleting a photo in Afterday deletes that copy.

Your report

The PDF report is made on your phone. It goes only where you send it from the share sheet, for example an email to your clinic. Whoever receives it handles it under their own policies.

What we collect

We collect nothing. No name, email, location, contacts or health data leave your phone, and nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. The store tells the app whether you have the Recovery Pass; we don't receive your payment details.

Reminders

Medication, drain and plan reminders are scheduled on your phone by iOS or Android. Their text never leaves the device.

Backups

If you use iCloud, Google or computer backups, your phone may include the app's data, photos included, in your own backup, covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data, photos included, from your phone.

Children

Afterday is for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.
