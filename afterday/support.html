How do I cancel?

The Recovery Pass doesn't renew, so there's nothing to cancel. For Monthly: on iPhone, Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Afterday; on Android, Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

I paid but the Recovery Pass isn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore, signed in with the Apple ID or Google account you paid with.

My surgery date or number of drains changed

Tap the settings button on the Today screen and change them. Your entries stay.

What is the surgeon's number?

Some surgeons give a drain amount in 24 hours to call them about. If yours did, enter it on the Drains screen and Afterday will show when your 24-hour total is at or under it. Only your surgeon decides when a drain comes out.

Where are my photos?

Inside Afterday only, not in your camera roll. Deleting the app deletes them, so save any you want to keep first.

Medical questions

Afterday is an organiser, not medical advice. Ask your surgeon or clinic.
