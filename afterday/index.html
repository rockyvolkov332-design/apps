Afterday

Surgery recovery, day by day.

A recovery log for cosmetic surgery: liposuction, tummy tuck, BBL, breast surgery, mommy makeover, arm and thigh lifts, and skin removal after weight loss. Count each day of recovery, record drain output with 24-hour totals, tick off medications, keep a private photo diary, put your surgeon's dates in one plan, and send your clinic a one-page report.

It records what your surgeon told you and what you measured. It never tells you when a drain can come out or what to take. No account; everything stays on your phone.
