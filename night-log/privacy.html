Privacy policy

Night Log: Bladder Diary · Updated 26 November 2026

The short version

Night Log keeps your diary on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ad trackers in the app. Nobody at Quietdry can see what you log.

What the app stores

The names you give each diary (for example "Me" or "Dad"), the times you log wees, drinks, leaks and pad changes, any amounts, urgency, leak and note you add, your usual bedtime and waking time, your unit choice (ml or oz), your reminder settings and, if you own Quietdry products, your descaler and sensor-tip dates. All of it is saved only in the app's own storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. No name, email, location, contacts, health data or device identifiers are sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no sign-in.

The report for your doctor

The PDF report is made on your device. It leaves the device only if you choose to share, print, save or send it, and then it goes only where you send it.

Reminders

Bedtime, morning, descaler and sensor reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS or Android. Their text never leaves the device.

Links to quietdry.com

Some buttons open quietdry.com in your browser. Those links carry a tag (utm_source=nightlog) so we know the visit came from the app; they carry nothing you logged. Once you are on the website, the quietdry.com privacy policy applies.

Backups

If you use iCloud, Google or computer backups, your device may include the app's data in your own backup, covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

Children

Night Log is made for adults and the people who care for them. It is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

More › Delete everything on this phone removes all diaries and settings at once. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from the device.

Not a medical device

Night Log is a diary for your own records and for sharing with a doctor or continence nurse. It does not diagnose or treat anything.
