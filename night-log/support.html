Which day does a night trip count for?

A diary day runs from waking to waking. Trips after bedtime and the first wee of the morning belong to the evening before, which is how doctors read a bladder diary.

How do I send the report to my doctor?

Diary › Share the report. Pick Mail, AirDrop, Print or Save to Files. The PDF covers the 3-day diary or the last 7 days.

Can I keep a diary for someone else?

Yes. Add a person in onboarding or with the For ▾ switch at the top. Each person has their own diary and report.

Reminders don't arrive

Check that notifications are allowed for Night Log in your phone's Settings, then turn the reminders off and on again in More.

New phone

Your diary moves with a full device backup or transfer. There is no account, so it can't be restored any other way.

Medical questions

Night Log is a diary, not medical advice. Ask your doctor or continence nurse about symptoms, fluids and treatment.
